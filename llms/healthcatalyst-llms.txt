# Health Catalyst

> Health Catalyst is a healthcare data, analytics, and AI company that helps healthcare organizations achieve measurable clinical, financial, and operational improvement. Its flagship platform, Health Catalyst Ignite, is a healthcare-specific data and analytics ecosystem built on Azure Databricks with pre-built standard APIs, healthcare data models, and self-service tools. Health Catalyst serves 1,100+ organizations. Backed in the API Evangelist network via Norwest Venture Partners.
